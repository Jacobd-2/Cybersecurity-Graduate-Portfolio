## Overview

This folder contains completion evidence for the **Network Defense Essentials (NDE) v1** training activities.

Because the training environment did not provide a separate certificate or transcript, screenshots captured from the training environment have been compiled into a single PDF for portfolio documentation.

**[View NDE v1 Completion Evidence](./Network-Defense-Essentials-NDE-v1-Completion-1-10.pdf)**

## Purpose

The evidence is included to provide recruiters and hiring managers with supporting documentation of the training activities represented in the **Network Defense Essentials** portfolio section.

The screenshots are presented as evidence of training activity completion and are not intended to reproduce or distribute EC-Council course materials.
