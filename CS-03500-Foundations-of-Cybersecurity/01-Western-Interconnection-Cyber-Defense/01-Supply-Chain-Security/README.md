## Project Overview

This phase evaluated cybersecurity risks affecting the **Western Interconnection power grid**, with a focus on supply chain security, third-party software, Industrial Control Systems (ICS), and supply chain visibility.

The assessment examined how weaknesses within critical infrastructure supply chains could introduce cybersecurity risks and evaluated security practices for reducing the potential impact of attacks against power-grid operations.

# Project Objectives

* Identify cybersecurity risks affecting critical infrastructure supply chains
* Evaluate risks associated with SCADA and Industrial Control Systems
* Examine third-party software and vendor security risks
* Evaluate supply chain visibility and transparency
* Research cybersecurity and threat-intelligence resources
* Develop recommendations for improving critical infrastructure security

# Security Risk Assessment

## 1. SCADA and Industrial Control System Security

SCADA and Industrial Control Systems are essential to monitoring and controlling power-grid operations.

A compromise of administrative access could allow attackers to manipulate control commands and potentially disrupt grid operations.

### Security Considerations

* Implement strong access controls
* Protect administrative accounts
* Apply Multi-Factor Authentication where appropriate
* Segment critical ICS environments
* Monitor access to operational systems
* Restrict unauthorized control commands

---

## 2. Third-Party Software and Vendor Risk

Third-party software can introduce significant cybersecurity risks into critical infrastructure environments.

The **SolarWinds breach** was examined as an example of how compromise of trusted third-party software can create downstream security risks for organizations using affected products.

### Security Considerations

* Evaluate third-party security practices
* Conduct security assessments of vendors
* Establish cybersecurity requirements for suppliers
* Test third-party software before deployment
* Monitor vendor security risks
* Maintain supply chain risk-management procedures

---

## 3. Supply Chain Visibility and Transparency

Limited visibility into suppliers and third-party vendors can make it difficult to identify cybersecurity weaknesses throughout the supply chain.

Smaller suppliers and organizations with limited cybersecurity resources may introduce risks that are difficult for larger organizations to identify without effective supply chain monitoring and collaboration.

### Security Considerations

* Maintain visibility into suppliers and vendors
* Identify critical third-party dependencies
* Conduct supplier risk assessments
* Establish communication and reporting procedures
* Monitor cybersecurity risks throughout the supply chain
* Apply security requirements throughout the technology lifecycle

# Security Recommendations

Based on the assessment, critical infrastructure organizations should:

* Strengthen security controls around SCADA and ICS environments
* Implement strong authentication and access controls
* Evaluate third-party software before deployment
* Conduct ongoing vendor and supplier risk assessments
* Improve visibility across the supply chain
* Establish cybersecurity requirements for suppliers
* Monitor vulnerabilities and emerging threats
* Maintain communication between organizations and suppliers
* Incorporate cybersecurity throughout the technology lifecycle
