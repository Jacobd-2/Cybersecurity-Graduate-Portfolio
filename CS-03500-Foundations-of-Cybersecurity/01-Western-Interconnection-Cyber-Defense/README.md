# Western Interconnection Cyber Defense

**Project Type:** Academic Cybersecurity Simulation
**Course:** Foundations of Cybersecurity — CS 03500
**Focus:** Critical Infrastructure Security, Threat Analysis, Cyber Defense

## Project Overview

This project simulated a cybersecurity engagement focused on defending the Western Interconnection power grid against a sophisticated foreign government-sponsored cyber threat.

The engagement was structured from the perspective of a security analyst working for a simulated U.S. Department of Defense contractor responsible for developing a defensive strategy for critical infrastructure.

The project evaluated the threat across multiple stages, from supply chain risk and threat intelligence through adversary analysis, Cyber Kill Chain analysis, defense in depth, mission assurance, and executive-level recommendations.

---

## Objectives

* Identify cybersecurity risks within critical infrastructure supply chains
* Analyze potential cyber threats against power grid environments
* Develop an adversary profile
* Apply the Cyber Kill Chain to a simulated malware scenario
* Evaluate defense-in-depth security controls
* Examine mission assurance and operational procedures
* Develop recommendations for improving cyber resilience
* Communicate technical security findings to executive leadership

---

## Project Phases

### 1. Supply Chain Security & Threat Intelligence

Evaluated cybersecurity risks affecting electricity generators and power grid infrastructure.

Key areas included:

* Supply chain security management
* Third-party risk
* Threat intelligence
* Vulnerability information
* Cybersecurity information sources

---

### 2. Threat Analysis & Cyber Kill Chain

Analyzed a simulated state-sponsored cyber threat targeting critical infrastructure.

Activities included:

* Adversary profiling
* Threat capability analysis
* Attacker objectives
* Malware analysis
* Cyber Kill Chain mapping
* Vulnerability identification

---

### 3. Defense in Depth

Evaluated multiple defensive layers designed to prevent, detect, and contain attacks.

Areas included:

* Network security controls
* Access controls
* Cryptographic protections
* Intrusion prevention
* Security monitoring
* Information assurance

---

### 4. Mission Assurance

Examined operational procedures and security technologies supporting the continued operation of critical infrastructure.

Topics included:

* Industrial control systems
* Firewalls
* Intrusion prevention systems
* SIEM
* Network defense
* Mission assurance
* Operational security procedures

---

### 5. Executive Security Briefing

Developed an executive-level presentation summarizing the assessment and recommended security strategy.

The presentation was designed for both technical personnel and nontechnical Department of Defense leadership.
