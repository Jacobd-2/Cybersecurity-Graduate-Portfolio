# Western Interconnection Cyber Defense

**Project:** Western Interconnection Cyber Defense
**Course:** CS 03500 — Foundations of Cybersecurity
**Project Type:** Academic Cybersecurity Simulation
**Focus:** Critical Infrastructure Security, Threat Analysis, Cyber Defense

## Overview

This project simulated a cybersecurity engagement focused on defending the Western Interconnection power grid against a sophisticated foreign government-sponsored cyber threat.

The engagement was developed from the perspective of a security analyst working for a simulated U.S. Department of Defense contractor responsible for developing a defensive strategy for critical infrastructure.

The assessment examined cybersecurity risks across four phases, progressing from supply chain security and threat intelligence to adversary analysis, Cyber Kill Chain analysis, defense in depth, and mission assurance.

## Objectives

The project focused on applying cybersecurity concepts to a simulated critical infrastructure environment.

Key objectives included:

* Identify cybersecurity risks within critical infrastructure supply chains
* Analyze potential cyber threats targeting power grid environments
* Develop an adversary profile and assess attacker capabilities
* Apply the Cyber Kill Chain to a simulated malware scenario
* Evaluate defense-in-depth security controls
* Examine Industrial Control System (ICS) security
* Evaluate mission assurance and network operational procedures
* Develop recommendations for improving cyber resilience

## Project Phases

### 1. Supply Chain Security & Threat Intelligence

Evaluated cybersecurity risks affecting power generation and grid infrastructure.

**Key areas:**

* Supply chain security
* Third-party and vendor risk
* SCADA/ICS security
* Threat intelligence
* Supply chain visibility
* Critical infrastructure protection

[View Supply Chain Security](./01-Supply-Chain-Security/)

### 2. Threat Analysis & Cyber Kill Chain

Analyzed a simulated state-sponsored cyber threat targeting critical infrastructure and mapped the attack lifecycle using the Cyber Kill Chain.

**Key areas:**

* Adversary profiling
* Threat capabilities and motivations
* Malware analysis
* Cyber Kill Chain
* Attack vectors
* ICS defensive strategies

[View Threat Analysis & Cyber Kill Chain](./02-Threat-Analysis-Cyber-Kill-Chain/)

### 3. Defense in Depth

Evaluated layered security controls designed to prevent, detect, and contain cyberattacks against critical infrastructure.

**Key areas:**

* Network segmentation
* Endpoint security
* Access control
* Multi-Factor Authentication
* Intrusion Detection and Prevention
* Data recovery
* Cryptographic protection

[View Defense in Depth](./03-Defense-in-Depth/)

### 4. Mission Assurance

Examined security and resilience strategies designed to maintain power grid operations during cyberattacks, physical disruptions, and system failures.

**Key areas:**

* Industrial Control Systems (ICS)
* Firewalls
* Intrusion Prevention Systems (IPS)
* Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
* Incident response
* Vulnerability and patch management
* Contingency planning
* Cyber resilience

[View Mission Assurance](./04-Mission-Assurance/)
