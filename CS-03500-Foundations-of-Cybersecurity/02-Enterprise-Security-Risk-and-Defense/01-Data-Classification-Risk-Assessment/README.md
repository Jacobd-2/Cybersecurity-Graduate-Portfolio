## Project Overview

This phase evaluated **data classification standards and information security risk assessment methodologies** for Fullsoft, Inc., a software development organization responsible for protecting sensitive business information and intellectual property.

The assessment examined data classification levels based on **sensitivity, access requirements, and business importance**, followed by an evaluation of established risk assessment methodologies for identifying and managing information security risks.

Two methodologies were evaluated: **NIST SP 800-30, Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments**, and **OCTAVE Allegro**.

Based on the organization's need to efficiently protect critical assets and intellectual property, **OCTAVE Allegro was recommended** as the preferred risk assessment methodology.

# Data Classification Standards

Data classification organizes information according to its **type, sensitivity, and importance**. Establishing classification levels helps organizations determine appropriate access controls and protection requirements.

The assessment developed four classification levels for Fullsoft, Inc.

## 1. Restricted Data

Restricted data represents highly sensitive information that should only be accessible to specifically authorized personnel.

### Security Considerations

* Limit access to authorized personnel
* Apply strong access controls
* Protect against unauthorized disclosure
* Monitor access to sensitive information
* Apply additional security protections where appropriate

---

## 2. Confidential Data

Confidential data includes organization-owned information such as **intellectual property** that requires authorization or clearance before access is granted.

### Security Considerations

* Require authorization before access
* Protect intellectual property
* Apply least-privilege access
* Monitor access to sensitive information
* Prevent unauthorized disclosure

---

## 3. Internal Data

Internal data consists of information intended for use within the organization and not for external distribution.

### Security Considerations

* Restrict unnecessary external disclosure
* Apply appropriate access controls
* Protect internal communications
* Establish information-handling procedures
* Educate employees on data-handling requirements

---

## 4. Public Data

Public data consists of information approved for unrestricted distribution and can be accessed by individuals inside or outside the organization.

### Security Considerations

* Verify information is approved for public release
* Maintain data accuracy
* Protect systems hosting public information
* Prevent unauthorized modification
* Apply appropriate integrity controls

# Risk Assessment Methodologies

## 1. NIST SP 800-30

**NIST SP 800-30, Guide for Conducting Risk Assessments**, provides a systematic approach for identifying, analyzing, and evaluating information security risks.

The methodology considers factors including **assets, threats, vulnerabilities, security controls, likelihood, and potential impact**.

### Key Considerations

* Asset identification
* Threat identification
* Vulnerability assessment
* Existing security controls
* Likelihood assessment
* Impact assessment
* Risk evaluation
* Risk mitigation

---

## 2. OCTAVE Allegro

**OCTAVE Allegro** is a streamlined information security risk assessment methodology focused on identifying and protecting critical information assets.

The approach emphasizes critical assets and business processes while providing a structured process for identifying threats, vulnerabilities, and potential risks.

### Key Considerations

* Identify critical information assets
* Define risk criteria
* Identify threats
* Identify vulnerabilities
* Evaluate potential risks
* Prioritize critical assets
* Develop mitigation strategies

---

# Risk Assessment Recommendation

## OCTAVE Allegro

The assessment recommended **OCTAVE Allegro** as the preferred risk assessment methodology for Fullsoft, Inc.

The recommendation was based on the organization's need to protect critical assets, particularly **intellectual property**, while maintaining an efficient risk assessment process.

OCTAVE Allegro provides a focused approach that prioritizes important business assets and processes while requiring fewer organizational resources than a more comprehensive assessment approach.

### Recommendation Considerations

* Focuses on critical information assets
* Supports protection of intellectual property
* Prioritizes essential business processes
* Provides a structured risk assessment process
* Can be conducted with limited resources
* Supports development of risk mitigation strategies

# Risk Assessment Comparison

| **Methodology**    | **Primary Focus**                                  | **Resource Consideration**                         |
| :----------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **NIST SP 800-30** | Comprehensive information security risk assessment | Greater personnel, time, and resource requirements |
| **OCTAVE Allegro** | Critical information assets and business risks     | Streamlined and resource-efficient                 |

For Fullsoft, the assessment determined that **OCTAVE Allegro provides a practical approach for prioritizing critical assets while reducing the resources required to conduct the assessment**.

# Key Security Takeaways

Key security priorities include:

* Data classification
* Information sensitivity
* Intellectual property protection
* Access control
* Asset identification
* Threat identification
* Vulnerability assessment
* Risk evaluation
* Risk prioritization
* Risk mitigation
* Information security governance

Establishing clear data classification standards allows organizations to apply security controls based on the sensitivity and importance of information.
