## Overview

Participated in the **National Cyber League (NCL) Spring 2025 Season**, a scenario-based cybersecurity competition designed to provide hands-on experience developing, practicing, and validating cybersecurity skills.

My participation included both the **Spring 2025 Individual Game** and **Spring 2025 Team Game**. The competition provided practical experience across multiple cybersecurity domains, including network traffic analysis, scanning and reconnaissance, enumeration and exploitation, password cracking, digital forensics, log analysis, web application exploitation, cryptography, and open source intelligence.

The NCL challenges are aligned with cybersecurity workforce competencies and performance-based objectives associated with industry certifications and the **NICE Cybersecurity Workforce Framework**.
