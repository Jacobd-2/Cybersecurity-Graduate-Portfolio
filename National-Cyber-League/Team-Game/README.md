## Overview

Participated as a member of team **cyber-class-SP25** in the **National Cyber League (NCL) Spring 2025 Team Game**, a real-time, scenario-based cybersecurity competition.

The Team Game emphasizes collaborative cybersecurity problem solving and evaluates technical skills across multiple security domains. Team members work together to analyze challenges, investigate evidence, identify vulnerabilities, and solve security problems under time constraints.

The competition provided practical exposure to enumeration and exploitation, digital forensics, password security, log analysis, network traffic analysis, reconnaissance, web application security, cryptography, and open source intelligence.

## Team-Based Cybersecurity Workflow

The Team Game required collaboration and prioritization while working through multiple concurrent cybersecurity challenges.

```text id="l7w2yq"
Challenge Identification
        ↓
Team Discussion & Prioritization
        ↓
Reconnaissance / Evidence Collection
        ↓
Technical Analysis
        ↓
Vulnerability or Threat Identification
        ↓
Investigation / Exploitation
        ↓
Result Validation
        ↓
Challenge Submission
        ↓
Team Review & Next Challenge
```

This environment reinforced the importance of communicating technical findings, dividing tasks, evaluating competing priorities, and adapting approaches during time-limited security investigations.
