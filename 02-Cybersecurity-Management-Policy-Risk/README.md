## Course Overview

This section presents cybersecurity management, governance, policy, risk assessment, business continuity, and security awareness projects developed as part of graduate-level cybersecurity coursework.

The projects demonstrate how cybersecurity extends beyond technical controls into **risk management, organizational governance, security policy development, regulatory compliance, workforce awareness, and operational resilience**.

The work is organized to demonstrate a complete security-management lifecycle:

```text
Identify Risks
      ↓
Develop Security Policies
      ↓
Assess & Prioritize Risk
      ↓
Establish Governance & Security Programs
      ↓
Plan for Business Disruption
      ↓
Train the Workforce
      ↓
Monitor, Review & Improve
```

The policy addresses:

* Visitor and contractor management
* Access privileges
* Badge expiration and renewal
* Lost or stolen badges
* Security awareness training
* Access logging
* Policy enforcement
* Effectiveness measurement

### Cybersecurity Concepts

* Policy Enforcement

## 02. Risk Assessment & Management

### Cybersecurity Audit & Risk Assessment

This project evaluates organizational security controls and identifies threats, vulnerabilities, and areas requiring remediation.

The assessment covers seven major security domains:

1. Personnel Security
2. Physical Security
3. Account & Password Management
4. Confidentiality of Sensitive Data
5. Disaster Recovery
6. Security Awareness & Education
7. Compliance & Audit

The project uses an impact-and-likelihood approach to calculate risk:

```text
Risk = Impact × Likelihood
```

The assessment also develops prioritized remediation actions for identified security weaknesses.

### Key Activities

* Security control assessment
* Threat identification
* Vulnerability analysis
* Impact assessment
* Likelihood assessment
* Risk scoring
* Risk prioritization
* Security remediation planning
* Security awareness recommendations
* Authentication and access-control recommendations
* Backup and recovery recommendations
* MFA recommendations

### Cybersecurity Concepts

* Risk Assessment
* Risk Management
* Threat & Vulnerability Analysis
* Security Controls
* Risk Prioritization
* Risk Treatment
* Remediation Planning
* Security Governance

### Portfolio Value

This project demonstrates the ability to identify organizational security risks, quantify risk, prioritize findings, and develop practical remediation strategies.

## 04. Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

### Business Continuity Plan for Green Foods Market

This project develops a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) for a simulated fresh-produce business operating both physical and online services.

The plan identifies critical business functions, establishes maximum tolerable downtime (MTD), evaluates disruption scenarios, and develops continuity strategies.

### Business Impact Analysis

| **Business Function**     | **Maximum Tolerable Downtime** |
| ------------------------- | -----------------------------: |
| Sales                     |                       24 Hours |
| Inventory Management      |                       48 Hours |
| Payment System            |                       24 Hours |
| Supply Chain              |                       72 Hours |
| Refrigeration             |                        8 Hours |
| Website & Online Ordering |                       24 Hours |

### Threat Scenarios

The BCP addresses:

1. Utility Disruption
2. Network Infrastructure Failure / Cloud Service Outage
3. Store Unavailability
4. Supply Chain Disruption
5. Data Loss / Cybersecurity Incident

### Continuity Strategies

Examples include:

* Backup generators
* Backup POS capabilities
* Backup laptops
* Refrigerated transportation
* Alternative ordering processes
* Rental delivery vehicles
* Alternative suppliers
* System recovery procedures
* Incident containment
* Log and security-alert review

### Program Maintenance

The plan also establishes:

* Emergency declaration procedures
* Emergency communication procedures
* Employee training
* Annual plan review
* Bi-annual testing
* Version control
* Lessons-learned activities
* Continuous improvement

### Portfolio Value

This project demonstrates the ability to connect **cybersecurity risk management with business resilience and operational continuity**.
