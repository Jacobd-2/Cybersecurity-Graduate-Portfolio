## Project Overview

This project developed a security awareness training presentation focused on the **Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA)** and the protection of healthcare information.

The training was designed to help employees understand privacy and security responsibilities when handling **Protected Health Information (PHI)** and **electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI)**.

The project combines cybersecurity awareness, regulatory requirements, risk management, and employee security responsibilities into an accessible training format.

The presentation covers HIPAA requirements, privacy and security safeguards, breach notification responsibilities, common security risks, and the potential consequences of non-compliance.

## Project Objectives

* Explain the purpose and importance of HIPAA
* Identify organizations and individuals subject to HIPAA requirements
* Distinguish between PHI and ePHI
* Explain the HIPAA Privacy Rule
* Explain the HIPAA Security Rule
* Explain the HIPAA Breach Notification Rule
* Introduce the Omnibus Rule and business associate responsibilities
* Identify administrative, physical, and technical safeguards
* Promote secure handling of sensitive healthcare information
* Increase employee awareness of cybersecurity threats
* Explain potential consequences of HIPAA violations
* Reinforce the importance of reporting suspected security incidents

## Training Topics

### 1. What Is HIPAA?

Introduces HIPAA and explains its role in protecting sensitive healthcare information and establishing requirements for covered entities and business associates.

### 2. Who Must Comply?

Explores the types of organizations and entities subject to HIPAA requirements, including covered entities and applicable business associates.

### 3. HIPAA Compliance Requirements

Introduces the major areas organizations must address when establishing a HIPAA security and privacy program, including:

* Risk analysis
* Administrative safeguards
* Physical safeguards
* Technical safeguards
* Privacy protections
* Security policies and procedures
* Workforce awareness and training
* Incident and breach response

### 4. Privacy Rule

Examines how the Privacy Rule establishes requirements for protecting individuals' health information and controlling how protected information may be used and disclosed.

### 5. Security Rule

Explains the Security Rule's focus on protecting **ePHI** through three safeguard categories:

* Administrative safeguards
* Physical safeguards
* Technical safeguards

### 6. Breach Notification Rule

Explains organizational responsibilities surrounding breaches of unsecured protected health information, including assessment, notification, documentation, and response considerations.

### 7. Omnibus Rule

Introduces changes that expanded HIPAA responsibilities, including requirements affecting business associates and their relationships with covered entities.

### 8. Consequences of HIPAA Violations

Discusses the potential organizational and individual consequences associated with HIPAA violations, including financial penalties, reputational damage, corrective actions, and increased regulatory scrutiny.

## Cybersecurity Concepts Demonstrated

### Security Awareness

Demonstrates how employee education can reduce security risks associated with human error, social engineering, phishing, improper information handling, and unauthorized disclosure.

### Data Protection

Emphasizes protecting sensitive healthcare information throughout its lifecycle.

### Administrative Safeguards

Addresses organizational policies, procedures, workforce responsibilities, training, and risk-management activities.

### Physical Safeguards

Addresses physical protections designed to prevent unauthorized access to systems and information containing ePHI.

### Technical Safeguards

Addresses technology-based protections used to control access to and protect electronic healthcare information.

### Risk Management

Connects HIPAA compliance activities to cybersecurity risk identification, assessment, mitigation, and continuous improvement.

### Incident Response

Highlights the importance of identifying, reporting, investigating, containing, and responding to potential security incidents and breaches.
